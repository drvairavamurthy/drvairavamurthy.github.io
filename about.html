About Dr. Vairavamurthy

Interventional radiologist and faculty member at Mount Sinai, focused on outpatient embolization and image-guided therapy in New York City.

Request a consultation Back to home

Dr. Vairavamurthy is an interventional radiologist and a faculty member at Mount Sinai. Interventional radiologists are physicians who use real-time imaging, such as X-ray and ultrasound, to guide thin catheters and needles to exactly where they are needed. That allows many conditions to be treated through a small puncture in the skin instead of an open operation.

Dr. Vairavamurthy’s practice focuses on outpatient embolization for conditions of the prostate, knees, shoulders, hips, feet and other painful joints and tendons, uterus, pelvis, rectum and gonads, along with interventional oncology care, including ports and image-guided tumor treatments, the treatment of blood clots, vein blockages and IVC filters, and bone and spine procedures such as kyphoplasty.

After training in New York and Los Angeles, Dr. Vairavamurthy went to Johns Hopkins on an NIH T32 fellowship as an investigator in bariatric embolization. That work earned the Society of Interventional Radiology’s 2017 Research of the Year award, added to the set of tools available for treating obesity, and solidified Dr. Vairavamurthy’s role as an expert in embolization. Dr. Vairavamurthy has published or presented on more than 75 studies, including work in major academic journals.

Dr. Vairavamurthy has since contributed at major academic centers in New York and Los Angeles, seeing and managing the most complicated of cases. Today, the skillset and confidence to help treat patients come with an equal commitment to avoiding harm, through meticulous planning and by steering clear of risky treatment strategies.

Dr. Vairavamurthy has a special passion for AI implementation and stays active in research. In free time, Dr. Vairavamurthy is a founding team member of an AI radiology startup.

Education is central to the work. Dr. Vairavamurthy is actively involved in teaching residents and medical students, takes great enjoyment in it, and has taken on important roles in shaping future physicians.

Dr. Vairavamurthy also cares deeply about the well-being of patients and physicians in a challenging health care world, where patients often do not know where to go for answers, are inundated with information, and lack appropriate outlets for guidance. Regardless of the issue, Dr. Vairavamurthy can help guide your care and form a long-term partnership.

Outside of work, Dr. Vairavamurthy enjoys mountain biking, exercise, golf and basketball, and spending time with family: a wife and two boys.
