Interventional Radiology · New York City

Image-guided therapy, as an outpatient.

Dr. Jenanan Vairavamurthy is an interventional radiologist who navigates the small blood vessels that feed the problem to treat common conditions including BPH, symptomatic uterine fibroids, joint pain, bleeding hemorrhoids, pelvic pain, infertility, tumors, blood clots and DVT, and leg swelling. A catheter is guided through a small puncture. Care also includes interventional oncology, from port placement to tumor treatments.

The best part: no cuts. Go home a couple of hours later with just a bandaid.
