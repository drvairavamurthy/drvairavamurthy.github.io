Are there side effects or complications?

Side effects are usually minimal and short-lived, such as mild soreness or bruising where the catheter went in. Serious complications are uncommon. Every procedure carries some risk, and the specific risks for your procedure are reviewed with you at your consultation.

Is the procedure painful?

The area is numbed and you are given light sedation, so most people feel pressure rather than pain. Some soreness afterward is common, and what to expect is covered at your consultation.

Will I need to stay overnight?

These procedures are done as outpatient treatments, and most people go home the same day. Your own plan depends on the procedure and your health.

Is embolization a replacement for surgery?

For some people it is an alternative to surgery, and for others it is used alongside other treatment. The consultation is where you find out which applies to you.

Is it covered by insurance?

Coverage varies by procedure and by plan. Insurance policies are reviewed for each patient.
